FEEDBACK & LEARNING

The power of feedback

Hattie, J., & Timperley, H. (2007). The power of feedback. Review of Educational Research, 77(1), 81–112.

A peer-reviewed review of feedback research that explains why feedback can influence learning and why its effects depend on the type and delivery of feedback.

Shute, V. J. (2008). Focus on formative feedback. Review of Educational Research, 78(1), 153–189.

A source appearing on this page does not mean it has already been cited in the written assignment. Make sure every reference is relevant to the final report and that in-text citations match the reference list.
