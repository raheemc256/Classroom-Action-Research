WHAT IS REQUIRED?

The evidence
toolkit.

Use the categories below to check that the collection stage is prepared before observations begin.

01 / INFORMATION
⌕

What information is required?

  • Background information related to the identified problem.
  • Details about the context in which the problem occurs.
  • Information from participants directly involved in the situation.
  • Existing records, documents, or previous findings related to the issue.
02 / INSTRUMENTS
▤

What are the tools required?

  • Observation checklists and recording sheets.
  • Surveys, questionnaires, or interview schedules.
  • Field notes and data recording tools.
  • Access to participants and relevant documents.
  • Digital tools for organising and analysing collected data.
03 / DATA
◎

What are the data required?

  • Qualitative data such as observations, opinions, experiences, and participant feedback.
  • Quantitative data such as frequencies, percentages, scores, or survey results.
  • Evidence that accurately reflects the nature and extent of the problem being investigated.
04 / PROCESS
⌁

What are the different process involved in this research?

  • Identify what information needs to be gathered.
  • Select appropriate data collection methods.
  • Observe and record relevant behaviours, events, or situations.
  • Collect data through surveys, interviews, questionnaires, or document analysis.
  • Analyse the collected information to identify trends, patterns, and key findings.
  • Share findings with the groups responsible for planning and implementing actions.
