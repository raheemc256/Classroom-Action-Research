What is the Purpose of the Observation/Data Collection Stage?

The purpose of the observation and data collection stage in classroom action research is to systematically gather information about students’ learning experiences, classroom behaviours, participation, and responses to the intervention. It allows the researcher to monitor what occurs in the classroom and collect relevant evidence to understand how the intervention influences students’ learning and performance.

The primary goal of the observation and data collection stage in classroom action research (CAR) is to systematically gather factual, real-time evidence on student behavior, engagement, and teacher practices to see if an intervention is working.
02 / SIGNIFICANCE
↗

What is the Significance?

  • 01
    Shows change

    It enables the researcher to identify improvements in students’ critical thinking skills, especially their capacity to analyse information and provide evidence explanations while considering alternative viewpoints and defend their conclusions.

  • 02
    Identifies challenges

    Classroom observations can help the researcher assess whether Socratic questioning and scaffolded drafting activities are being implemented as intended. If the students demonstrate limited engagement or struggle to justify their responses, the researcher can identify these difficulties and make appropriate adjustments to the intervention.

  • 03
    Guides decisions

    Helps the teacher-researcher decide whether to maintain or adjust teaching strategies.

  • 04
    Supports reflection

    The observation stage can provide evidence that can be compared with other data sources, such as student’s written work and feedback. This strengthens the overall interpretation of the findings and help the researcher to develop more understanding of student’s learning experiences.
