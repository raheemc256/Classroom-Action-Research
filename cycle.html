STAGE 03 / SELECTED STAGE

03

Observation &
data collection

The researcher records what students do, say, and produce while the intervention is taking place. This creates evidence for reflection instead of relying on impressions alone.

↔
Connects action to reflection

It captures the effects of the intervention and provides material to interpret during reflection.

↻

The cycle is iterative. Reflection may lead to a revised plan, a new action, and another round of observation. The stages work together rather than operating as isolated tasks.

05 / OBSERVATION STAGERecords what happens and analyzes it

This stage supplies the evidence that links the intervention to Reflection.

06 / REFLECTION STAGETurns evidence into decisions

Observation supplies the evidence. Reflection interprets it and judges whether the intervention was effective.
